Frequently Asked Questions

Are your estimates truly free and upfront?

Yes! For standard residential and commercial upgrades (such as EV chargers, panel replacements, and lighting retrofits), our estimates are 100% free with transparent flat-rate pricing before work begins.

How fast can an emergency electrician arrive?

Our average dispatch time is 30 to 45 minutes across Metro City and surrounding suburbs. Our service vans are fully stocked with standard circuit breakers, wire, and emergency disconnects.

Do you pull the required city building permits?

Always. As licensed C-10 Electrical Contractors, we handle the entire permitting process with the municipal building department, schedule required inspections, and coordinate with the electric utility company.
