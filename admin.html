EmailJS Instant Lead & Estimate Alerts

Ready to Connect

Automatically send an instant email to ekdantenterprise33@gmail.com whenever a customer submits an appeal for a quote, estimate, or service.

How to get these 3 keys from EmailJS (Step-by-Step Instructions) Click to expand ▾
1
Sign Up / Log In: Go to https://www.emailjs.com/ and create a free account.
2
Add Email Service: Click Email Services → Add New Service → choose Gmail → connect ekdantenterprise33@gmail.com. Copy the generated Service ID (e.g. service_xxxxx) into the box above.
3
Create Email Template: Click Email Templates → Create New Template. In the template settings, set To Email to ekdantenterprise33@gmail.com (or {{to_email}}). Set Subject to ⚡ New Estimate Request: {{service_requested}} - {{customer_name}}. Copy the Template ID (e.g. template_xxxxx) into the box above.
4
Get Public Key: Click Account (bottom left) → API Keys → copy your Public Key into the box above.
5
Save & Test: Click Save EmailJS Settings above, then click Send Test Email. You will immediately receive a test notification in your Gmail inbox!
